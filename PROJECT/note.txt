| Flowchart     | Rumus                   | Kode                                  |

| CNN           | Convolution, ReLU       | `Conv2D`                              |

| Backprop      | gradien                 | `optimizer='adam'`                    |
| Evaluasi      | CM, Acc, dll            | `confusion_matrix()`                  |
